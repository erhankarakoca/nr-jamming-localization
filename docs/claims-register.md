### Ten most serious problems

1. **Other receivers did protect the link against hopping.** The hard power-threshold map reduced hopping-data BLER from 29.25% to 0.95% on 1,152 common slots. The mixed-attack U-Net’s distinction was near-oracle BLER on **both** hopping cases. The papers’ “only” claim overstates that distinction. M06, M90, C04.
2. **The site’s recovery headline spans cases where the nominal U-Net leaves substantial BLER.** At +9 dB JSR it leaves 21.2% on hopping data and 31.3% on hopping partial-band jamming. The headline’s near-elimination of block errors applies to the nominal recovery experiment. S08.
3. **The recovery headline needs its operating point attached.** The observed 4/4,800 maximum belongs to nominal attacks on four of 12 scheduled PRBs, TDL-C, 15 dB SNR, and known noise power. With estimated noise and the whole allocation’s DMRS jammed, the true-map receiver had 344/640 errors versus 340/640 for standard decoding at +3 dB JSR. M82, M87, C03, S19.
4. **The detection margin is a difference in JSR thresholds, and its sign changes with SNR.** The smallest TDL-C margin at the 1% slot-FA target is +8.61 dB at 15 dB SNR, +3.48 dB at 8 dB, and −6.05 dB at 5 dB. The 7.3–9.1 dB interval belongs to the 15 dB TDL-C minimum. M05, M63–M66, C31, S18.
5. **Several “1% false alarm” labels mix calibration, test measurements, and a test-label comparison.** The main detector’s held-out all-cell FA is 1.1522%. Ordinary thresholds target 1% of scored clean validation cells. The matched comparison uses test labels and a whole-cell budget per attack. M22, M59–M60, C11, C29–C30, S17.
6. **The exact CFAR and replay statements depend on the retained subspace.** A finite delay bound gives an approximation. Exact results require the channel component to lie in the retained span, with the stipulated white noise and known noise power. Long-delay TDL-C produced 62.86% residual FA, and partial replay produced residual flags on clean neighbors. M02, M36, M38, M54, M73–M74, C20.
7. **The 0.48 ms result is a post-equalization detector benchmark.** It is the sum of stage medians for the frozen 7,657-parameter model. The frozen 66,553-parameter model totals 0.684 ms, and the separate eager benchmark totals 0.817 ms. The benchmarks exclude receiver processing and decoding from the timed stages. M80, C37, L13.
8. **The novelty exclusion is broader than the evidence.** Prior art already covers symbol-by-RB EVM, pilot-subspace detection, and localized OFDM pilot erasure and interference-aware LLRs. The defensible contribution is the specific PRB-indexed residual law, feature fusion, and measured SISO NR receiver integration. M09, M12–M13, C06–C07. [Örnek and Kartal](https://research.itu.edu.tr/en/publications/an-advanced-evm-metric-based-jamming-signal-detection-method-for-/), [Mehrabian and Kaddoum](https://doi.org/10.1109/LCOMM.2026.3697967), [Li et al.](https://www.merl.com/publications/docs/TR2012-084)
9. **The receiver supplies the observations used to construct the features.** The detector adds raw individual-pilot LS calculations, the delay projection, and decision-directed EVM. The README diagram also places carrier-wide power under the scheduled-PRB crop. M01, M23, C42, R02, R09.
10. **The conference text overstates the limits of no-map LMMSE.** At +9 dB it improved BLER on the common slots by 5.73 percentage points for pulsed partial-band jamming, 1.39 for hopping data, and 7.47 for hopping partial-band. All three paired intervals exclude zero. C38.

### Evidence and checks

`J/` means `src/jamloc/`, `R/` means `results/`, and `D/` means `results/site/build/data/`. Evidence references give physical file lines or a CSV key where a table spans many rows. `main.tex` and `conference.tex` refer to the sibling `nr-jamming-paper` checkout. `FA` is false-alarm rate, `Pd` is detection probability, and `pp` is percentage points.

The audit used the complete paper, README and scoped website sources, the results log, the 17 November literature audit, source and tests, and saved result artifacts. The website source was read with `git show` at `compact-site` commit `26e6a3f`. Its scoped HTML and JS matched `gh-pages`. All 1,051 aggregate JSON rows were reconciled to CSVs, and the 13 downloads, aggregate JSON, and 12 case JSONs were checked against their saved counterparts. Numerical checks included NPZ populations, residual eigenspectra and tails, margin crossings, and pooled block counts.

**Checks that could not be completed.** The full simulation, training, timing and deployment runs were outside the read-only audit. Selected tests were inspected, but their execution stopped during collection when Matplotlib needed a writable cache. Live Pages access, authenticated Overleaf state, historical runtime estimates, several reported bootstrap intervals, the raw array behind one block-test diagnostic, and exhaustive prior-art exclusion remain unverified. The full Mehrabian and Örnek–Kartal papers were available locally and were checked despite the access gaps recorded in the November literature audit.
