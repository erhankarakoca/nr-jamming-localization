Localizing jamming in the 5G uplink

For every uplink slot, a small U-Net marks the resource cells (PRB × OFDM symbol) a jammer transmitted on, from three receiver measurements: received power P, pilot residual R, and error vector magnitude E. The receiver then decodes the same buffered slot again with the map.
