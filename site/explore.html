The resource grid

Rows are physical resource blocks (PRBs), columns are OFDM symbols, and symbol 2 carries the pilots (DMRS). The colour scale stays fixed while the power changes.

Choose a cell to inspect it; the arrow keys move between cells when the grid has focus.

Selected cell

Equalized symbols

Red points come from jammed cells and blue points from clean cells; the paler a point, the less the decoder trusts its bits. The map-aware receiver uses the U-Net's map, and the CRC checks the whole decoded block.
