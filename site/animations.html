Pilot residual vs. JSR

The pilot residual is the part of the channel estimate left after projection onto the delay subspace. The normalized residual Rb combines pilot residual power within PRB b.

As in Fig. 3, the top chart divides each pilot’s residual power by the noise variance of the least-squares channel estimate before converting to dB. This variance is N0/Pp, where N0 = 10−SNR/10 uses the case SNR in dB and Pp = 2 is the energy per occupied DMRS RE for the configured two CDM groups.
