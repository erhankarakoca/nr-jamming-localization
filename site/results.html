Detection probability

For pilot jamming at −15 dB JSR, adding the residual to power and EVM raises the share of jammed pilot cells detected from 39.4% to 57.4%.

How this was measured

Detection margin

JSR at which the standard receiver reaches 10% BLER minus the JSR at which 90% of jammed slots are detected: positive means the detector warns first. It is positive at 15, 11 and 8 dB SNR and negative at 5 dB. Open markers are upper bounds.

How this was measured

Block error rate at +9 dB JSR

Against the nominal jammer the U-Net map leaves at most 0.06% BLER (TDL-C, 15 dB SNR, known noise power). Against a hopping jammer, which it was never trained on, it leaves 21% (data) and 31% (partial-band) when the receiver averages the jamming power over a PRB. Estimated per cell, with passes that refine the map from the decoder's output, it leaves none in 4,800 slots and 0.25%, the same as the true map and as a network retrained on mixed attacks.

How this was measured

Localization after decoding

Once a slot decodes, every data symbol is known, and each cell gets a test whose false-alarm rate is set by an exact law, whatever the shape of the attack.
